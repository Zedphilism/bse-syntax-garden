Common mistakes — avoid these

  • Forgetting saturation self-loops (X=0 at 111, X=1 at 000)
  • Wrong Gray code order in K-maps (must be 00 01 11 10, NOT 00 01 10 11)
  • Not treating JK don't-cares as 1s when grouping K-map cells
  • Wiring CLK directly to flip-flops instead of through CLK_EN gate
  • Using free-running clock instead of push button
  • Submitting D flip-flop design instead of JK
